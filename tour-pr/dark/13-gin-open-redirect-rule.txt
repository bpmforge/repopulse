What to do

Request data decides where the user is redirected. A link to your own domain can then send visitors to another site: a path that starts with `//` or `/\` is read by browsers as a host. Redirect only to a path that starts with a single `/` followed by something other than `/` or `\`, or to an allowlisted host.
