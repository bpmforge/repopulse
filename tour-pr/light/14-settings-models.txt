Quality per model

How each model did on labelled test cases, per role. Precision (P): of what it reported, how much was real. Recall (R): of the planted defects, how many it found.

Reading it: each role has only four cases, so one case moves a number by 25 points or more; read a gap smaller than that as a tie. For Security and Triage, low recall matters most (a missed real issue); for the other roles, low precision means noise to wade through. To use a model for a role, put it first in that role’s chain in Routing.

The cases are small and their labels have not been reviewed by a person: a rough guide, not a guarantee. “Not yet run” means nothing was measured.
