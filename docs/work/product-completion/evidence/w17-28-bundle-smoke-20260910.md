```
      exit=1   (nothing loaded, nothing started)
```

A third failure was found by the smoke itself, not staged: the FIRST attempt
used the default compose project `repopulse`, whose `repopulse_pgdata` volume
still existed from the 2026-08-12 W11-01 smoke under a different password.
`up.sh` reported `DATABASE MIGRATION FAILED (exit 1) … Nothing else was
started against it on purpose` and pointed at the log (28P01 `auth_failed`).
That is clear about *what*, not *why*, so `up.sh` now greps the migrate log
for 28P01 and adds the cause and the two ways out (restore the matching
`.env`, or remove the volume knowingly). Not re-exercised live after the fix;
the hint path is guarded by the existing behaviour tests only.
